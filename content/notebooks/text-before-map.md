At a rural bus stop, a map that takes ten seconds to load is a bus you missed. RideBustang started from that sentence.

## The least we can send

We asked one question of every screen: what is the smallest payload that still gets someone on the right bus? Usually the answer was a time, a stop name and a walking distance — a few hundred bytes of text.

The map still exists. It just waits to be asked for.

> One answer, not a list.
